# Vellum 1.0

Version 1 is the reader you would actually use. It is an Android app, and the voice is a natural model that stays on the phone. 0.3.0 already has the reading. These builds close the rest. 1.0.0 is not cut until build 2 and build 3 both hold on a mid-range Android phone.

## Already required, and already in 0.3.0

- Open an epub. Keep the library and your place on the device.
- Read a page, open the contents, see progress.
- Font, size, spacing, margins, and paper, sepia, gray, or night.
- Read quietly, read along, or listen with the screen off.
- Mark the current sentence. Pause after a paragraph. Pause longer after a heading.
- Bookmarks.

## Out of 1.0

Accounts, sync, a store, notes, a dictionary, the book's own layout, and any language but English.

## Build 1 — listen session

`1.0.0-build.1`

- Sleep after 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or the end of the chapter. Pausing does not reset the clock.
- Lock-screen controls for play, pause, previous sentence, and next sentence, with the book and chapter name.
- The page and the listen screen still share one place in the book.

## Build 2 — natural voice

- One English model on the device. It is downloaded once, then it needs no network.
- Mara, Jonah, and Helen are that model, not the phone's system voice.
- The same pauses and the same sentence mark. The page stays responsive while a sentence is prepared.
- If the model is missing, say so. The page still opens. Do not call the system voice the natural one.

## Build 3 — Android

- An installable app.
- Open an epub from the phone.
- Playback continues with the screen locked, through the media notification.
- Headset and lock-screen buttons use the same previous, next, play, and pause.
- Books, place, bookmarks, and the voice model stay on the phone.

## 1.0.0

Build 2 and build 3, on a phone, without a hitch or a hot device through a chapter.
